<p>Arsu keeps your two-factor secrets in a single encrypted file on your phone and shows their codes. It never connects to the network: it has no internet permission, no account, no sync and no telemetry.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Encrypted vault.</b> Your master password is stretched with Argon2id (256 MiB, 3 passes), and the whole vault is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. The file is useless without the password.</li>
<li><b>Crash-safe saves</b> that never leave a half-written vault, plus the last three versions kept next to it, in case of a bad edit.</li>
<li><b>Add accounts</b> from an otpauth:// link, a picture of a QR code, or by hand.</li>
<li><b>TOTP and HOTP</b>, SHA-1/256/512, 6–8 digits, with the next code shown before the current one expires.</li>
<li><b>Fingerprint and face unlock</b> (strong biometrics), with a key kept in the phone's secure hardware that adding a fingerprint or face destroys.</li>
<li><b>Locks itself</b> when the screen goes off, and after an idle time you choose.</li>
<li><b>Clears copied codes</b> from the clipboard after a delay you choose. Codes are marked sensitive, so the clipboard preview and keyboards don't show them.</li>
<li><b>Imports</b> Aegis and 2FAS backups, plain or encrypted, skipping accounts you already have. <b>Exports</b> an encrypted Aegis backup, or a single account as a QR code.</li>
<li><b>No screenshots</b> or screen recording of the app, and no cloud backup of its data.</li>
<li><b>Light and dark themes</b>, or following the system.</li>
</ul>

<p>Arsu is also available for the Linux desktop, from the same code.</p>
